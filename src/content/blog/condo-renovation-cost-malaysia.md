Search "condo renovation cost Malaysia" and most results give you a single number with no context, the same generic RM30,000 to RM100,000 range you would see for any property type. That range is not wrong, but it misses the part that actually makes condo renovation different from a landed house: strata rules. A condo renovation is not just a scope of work, it is a scope of work that has to clear your management corporation or JMB first, and that approval process shapes both the cost and the timeline in ways a terrace house renovation never has to account for.

This guide breaks down what condo renovation actually costs in Malaysia, using the same ranges we quote our own clients, and explains the strata-specific constraints, lift limits, wet-work rules, approval timelines, that a generic cost estimate leaves out entirely.

## Condo Renovation Cost by Scope

### Light refresh (painting, flooring, minor fittings)

**RM 15,000 to RM 35,000** [VERIFY: confirm this tier's range with Catherine before publishing]

A light refresh covers repainting, flooring replacement, and swapping out fittings and fixtures without touching plumbing layout, electrical wiring, or the unit's internal walls. Most management corporations treat this as non-structural and it typically clears approval quickly, sometimes with no more than a notification to the management office.

### Full interior renovation

**RM 40,000 to RM 90,000** [VERIFY: confirm this tier's range with Catherine before publishing]

A full interior renovation touches the kitchen, bathrooms, living and dining areas, and bedrooms, including new built-in carpentry, full tiling, and M&E works. Our Platinum Arena project in Subang Jaya sits in this tier: a full unit fit-out covering custom built-in carpentry throughout, tiling and epoxy flooring, plaster ceilings, and a complete M&E upgrade, delivered in one month once approvals and scheduling were in place. The Sierra 6 project in Puchong is a similar scale, focused on kitchen carpentry, bedroom built-ins, and a full electrical upgrade, also completed within a month.

### Full refurbishment with wet works

**RM 80,000 to RM 120,000** [VERIFY: confirm this tier's range with Catherine before publishing]

This tier covers a full renovation where every bathroom and the kitchen wet area are rebuilt, not just refinished, including waterproofing membrane, replumbing, and new tiling throughout. Our Sri Murni project along Old Klang Road in Kuala Lumpur falls in this range: every bathroom refurbished, both the wet and dry kitchen rebuilt with new plumbing, full M&E wiring, and full tiling and SPC flooring across the unit, delivered on a compressed 30-day timeline.

## What Actually Drives the Cost, Beyond Finishes

### Strata approval

Every condo renovation needs sign-off from the Joint Management Body or Management Corporation before work can start, even for scope that would need no approval at all in a landed house. Expect to submit a renovation plan, sometimes with a refundable deposit held against damage to common property, and to wait anywhere from a few days for minor works to several weeks for anything involving wet areas or structural elements. A contractor who has not budgeted time for this step in your project timeline has not planned the job properly. Our [guide to authority submission](/blog/authority-submission-renovation-malaysia/) covers the government-side approvals that can stack on top of strata sign-off when a renovation is large enough to need both.

### Lift and loading limits

Materials, debris, and equipment all move through the building's service lift, which has a fixed weight and size limit that a landed house renovation never has to work around. Bulky items like full sets of kitchen cabinetry or large format tiles sometimes need to be broken down for transport, and most buildings restrict renovation work to specific hours and specific lift access windows. This adds coordination time to the project, and a contractor unfamiliar with your building's rules will lose days figuring it out on site rather than before the quote.

### Wet-work restrictions

Waterproofing rules for high-rise bathrooms and kitchens are stricter than for a ground-floor unit, because a failed membrane does not just damage your own unit, it can leak into the unit below. Most management corporations require a minimum cure time and, in some buildings, a water-ponding test before work can be signed off and covered over with tiling. Budget RM8,000 to RM25,000 [VERIFY: confirm this range with Catherine] per wet area depending on size and whether the existing membrane needs full replacement, higher than the equivalent line item in a landed renovation because of the added compliance requirement.

### Material and access constraints

Condo corridors, lift lobbies, and loading bays are shared spaces, which means larger deliveries need scheduling in advance and sometimes protective covering for common areas, a cost a landed renovation simply does not have. Ask any quote whether it includes protection for common property and delivery coordination, since an unusually low quote is often one that has left this out.

## Why Strata Rules, Not Just Finishes, Set the Real Budget

Two condos with identical finish specifications can still land on very different final costs if one building has a slow, document-heavy approval process and the other does not. Before you compare quotes across contractors, confirm with your JMB or MC what their renovation approval process actually requires: how long it takes, whether a deposit is held, and what hours and lift access are permitted for renovation work. A contractor who has worked in your building before, or in a building with a similar management structure, can usually tell you what to expect before you even submit.

## Getting an Accurate Quote for Your Condo Renovation

1. Check your building's renovation guidelines with the JMB or MC before requesting quotes, so every contractor is pricing against the same access and timeline constraints
2. Confirm whether strata approval and any deposit are included in the quoted timeline, not treated as a separate step you have to manage yourself
3. Ask whether wet-area waterproofing and cure time are itemised, especially for a high-rise unit above ground floor
4. Get material grade specified by name or brand, the same as any other renovation, since this is where quotes for identical scope diverge most
